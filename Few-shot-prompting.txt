1. Definition

Few-Shot Prompting is a technique where we provide an AI with a few examples of a task before asking it to perform the same task on new information.

Purpose in AI

- Helps the AI understand what we want it to do.
- Shows the AI the expected pattern, format, or style.
- Helps produce more accurate and consistent responses.

2. How Does Few-Shot Prompting Work?

Few-Shot Prompting works in three simple steps:

1. Give the AI an instruction.
2. Provide a few examples showing the correct way to perform the task.
3. Give the AI a new input and ask it to produce the answer.

The AI studies the multiple examples and identifies the common pattern or relationship. It then applies that pattern to the new input.

3. Why Do Multiple Examples Help?

Multiple examples give the AI more information about the task.

- They show different possible situations.
- They make the expected pattern clearer.
- They reduce confusion caused by a single example.
- They help the AI produce more consistent responses.
- They are especially useful for tasks with specific rules or formats.

4. Common Applications

Few-Shot Prompting can be used for:

- Classification – Categorizing text or information.
- Sentiment analysis – Identifying positive, negative, or neutral opinions.
- Translation – Following a particular translation style.
- Summarization – Creating summaries in a specific format.
- Question answering – Following a desired answer pattern.
- Text extraction – Extracting specific information from text.
- Text formatting – Converting information into a particular format.

5. Example of Few-Shot Prompting

Prompt:

> Instruction: Classify each sentence as Positive or Negative.

Example 1:

Example 2:
"The food was terrible." → Negative

Example 3:
"The movie was fantastic." → Positive

New sentence:
"The service was excellent." → ?



AI Response:

> Positive



Here, the AI uses the three examples to understand the pattern and applies it to the new sentence.

6. Advantages

- Better guidance: Multiple examples clearly show what is expected.
- Improved accuracy: Can produce better results than zero-shot or one-shot prompting for some tasks.
- More consistent: Helps the AI follow a particular pattern or format.
- Handles complex tasks: Useful when instructions alone are not enough.
- Flexible: Can demonstrate different types of inputs and outputs.

7. Limitations

- Uses more tokens: Multiple examples make the prompt longer.
- Requires good examples: Poor examples can lead to poor results.
- Can be time-consuming: Creating suitable examples takes effort.
- Limited context: Too many examples can make the prompt unnecessarily large.
- May still make mistakes: Examples do not guarantee completely correct answers.

8. When Is Few-Shot Prompting More Useful?

Method	Best suited for

Zero-Shot	Simple and straightforward tasks where no example is needed
One-Shot	Tasks where one example is enough to show the expected pattern
Few-Shot	Tasks that are more complex or require a specific pattern, format, or style


Few-Shot Prompting is especially useful when one example does not provide enough information to understand the task, but a large number of examples is unnecessary.
